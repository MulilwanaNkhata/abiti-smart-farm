Our Farm

An integrated farm in the Thandaza area of Mzuzu, where crops, livestock, poultry and fish work together.

The farm occupies approximately three acres of family-owned land and has access to a small river, offering potential for improved irrigation and more consistent crop production.

Crop production

We grow vegetables and field crops for local and commercial markets.

  • Vegetables: carrots, tomatoes, green peppers, cabbages, beetroot, leafy vegetables and onions.
  • Field crops: maize, sweet potatoes, beans, groundnuts and peas.
  • We are also exploring commercial hibiscus production.

Our target customers include households, schools, hotels, restaurants, retailers and institutional buyers.

Pig farming

Pig production is part of our diversified farming enterprise and contributes to our goal of building a commercially viable integrated farm.

Developing

Poultry farming

We are developing our poultry enterprise, with an interest in local chicken production, particularly Mikolongwe black chickens.

Fish farming

We operate two fish ponds as part of our integrated farming model, contributing to diversified food production and potential income generation.

Poultry incubation services

We offer poultry incubation services and Mikolongwe local chicks for sale, subject to availability. Chick enquiries may include birds from day-old to four weeks old.

We aim to support other farmers by making local poultry and incubation services more accessible.

Emerging initiative

Alternative animal-feed innovation

We are exploring Azolla cultivation and black soldier fly production as potential supplementary feed resources. These initiatives aim to improve resource efficiency and explore locally available feed alternatives.

These projects are under development and are not yet fully established commercial operations.
