Our Farm

Integrated agricultural operations in Thandaza, Mzuzu, where crops, fish, pigs and local chickens are being connected into one resource-efficient system.

The farm occupies approximately three acres of family-owned land and has access to a small river, offering potential for improved irrigation and more consistent crop production.

B. Fish farming and aquaculture

Fish ponds

We operate two fish ponds as part of our integrated farming model, contributing to diversified food production and potential income.

Future development: we plan to grow our aquaculture enterprise and are exploring Black Soldier Fly larvae and Azolla as locally produced supplementary fish feed.

Operational

C. Pig farming

Pigs

Pig production is part of our diversified farming enterprise and contributes to our goal of building a commercially viable integrated farm.

Within our integrated approach, pig manure can be managed as an organic resource for recycling, and BSF larvae and Azolla are being explored as supplementary feed.

Operational

D. Local chicken farming

Local chickens and incubation

We raise local chickens and manage our flock through incubation and brooding. Local poultry can contribute to food production and household livelihoods in our community.

We offer Mikolongwe local chicks from day-old to four weeks old, subject to availability, and a poultry incubation service for other farmers.

Innovation

Black Soldier Fly farming

Our BSF setup is established. BSF larvae can convert suitable organic waste into useful agricultural resources: larvae as a potential protein ingredient for fish and livestock feed, and frass that can be explored as a soil amendment.
