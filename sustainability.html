How Abiti is developing a resource-efficient, integrated farming system where each activity can support the others.

We are connecting crop production, fish farming, pig farming and local chickens with new feed and recycling innovations, so that resources can be reused rather than wasted. A. Azolla cultivation

Developing Azolla as a feed resource

We are developing Azolla cultivation as part of our integrated farming approach. Azolla is a fast-growing aquatic fern, and it is not yet an established operation at Abiti.

B. Black Soldier Fly (BSF) farming

Our BSF setup is established

Abiti has established a Black Soldier Fly farming setup. BSF larvae feed on suitable organic material, and we are building this into our integrated farming system.

  • Waste to resourceConverting suitable organic waste into useful agricultural resources.
  • Protein feedProducing insect larvae as a potential protein ingredient for fish and livestock feed.
  • SoilProducing frass that can be explored as a soil amendment.
  • Circular farmingSupporting circular agriculture and resource efficiency.

Using larvae in feed and frass on our fields are potential applications that we are still exploring.
