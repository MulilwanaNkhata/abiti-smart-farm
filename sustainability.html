Sustainability and Innovation

Building an integrated farming system where each part supports the others.

Integrated farming

Crops, livestock, poultry, fish and feed working together

We aim to develop an integrated farming system in which crops, livestock, poultry, fish farming and alternative feed resources complement one another. We believe agriculture can contribute to commercial growth, environmental responsibility and community wellbeing.

Our priorities

Where we are focusing

Water management

Improving irrigation and water management, with access to a small river on the farm.

Responsible reuse

Exploring responsible reuse of suitable farm by-products.

Alternative feed

Developing alternative feed-production systems.

Livestock and infrastructure

Improving livestock management and farm infrastructure.

Records and planning

Strengthening production records and business planning.

Customer relationships

Building reliable relationships with the people we supply.

Emerging initiatives

Azolla and black soldier fly

We are exploring Azolla cultivation and black soldier fly production as potential supplementary feed resources. These initiatives aim to improve resource efficiency and explore locally available feed alternatives.

These projects are under development. They are emerging initiatives, not yet fully established commercial operations.

Always learning

Innovation at Abiti means trying better ways to produce food and manage farm resources, one step at a time, and sharing what we learn with other farmers and partners.
