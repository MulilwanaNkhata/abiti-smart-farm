Partnerships

We aim to build lasting relationships through quality, reliability, transparency and a shared commitment to growth.

Who we welcome

Enquiries and potential partnerships

  • Households and local produce buyers
  • Schools and educational institutions
  • Hotels, restaurants and food-service businesses
  • Farmers interested in chicks and incubation services
  • Agricultural organisations and research institutions
  • Development partners and grant-making organisations
  • Businesses interested in agricultural supply and collaboration
  • Community members interested in learning and future initiatives

Where we are heading

Our future plans

  • Expanding vegetable production and institutional supply
  • Improving irrigation infrastructure
  • Strengthening poultry and livestock production
  • Developing Azolla and black soldier fly production
  • Improving farm facilities and recordkeeping
  • Exploring agricultural value addition
  • Building partnerships with technical experts and development organisations
  • Creating opportunities for young people, women and other farmers
  • Developing community activities inspired by generosity and togetherness

Our goal is to build a farm that grows commercially while remaining connected to the people and community around it.
