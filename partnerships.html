How we can work together

Ways to partner with Abiti

Institutional supply arrangements

Regular supply of fresh vegetables and crops to schools, hotels, restaurants and retailers, agreed around the seasons.

Bulk produce enquiries

One-off or recurring bulk orders, confirmed against current availability.

Partnership proposals

Programmes in food security, women's economic empowerment, youth participation and farmer development.

Agricultural collaboration

Technical and research collaboration on Black Soldier Fly farming, Azolla, climate-smart agriculture and solar irrigation.

We are open to new partnerships. This page describes the kinds of organisations we would like to work with; it is not a list of current partners.

Where we are heading

Our future plans

Our goal is to build a farm that grows commercially while remaining connected to the people and community around it.

  • Expanding vegetable production and institutional supply
  • Solar-powered irrigation and better irrigation infrastructure (planned)
  • Strengthening fish, pig and local chicken production
  • Growing our established Black Soldier Fly setup
  • Completing the development of Azolla cultivation
  • Improving farm facilities and record-keeping
  • Exploring agricultural value addition
  • Building partnerships with technical experts and development organisations
  • Creating opportunities for women, young people and local farmers
