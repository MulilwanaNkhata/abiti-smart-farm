Community Impact and Development

Our commitment to inclusive agricultural development, inspired by a legacy of generosity.

Working with local farmers

Growing opportunity together

We intend to work with and support local farmers through knowledge-sharing, practical agricultural training and potential market opportunities. Inspired by our grandmother's generous spirit, we want Abiti to be a welcoming place where people can connect, learn and grow.

Our focus areas

The impact we are working towards

Women's economic empowerment

Opening income and leadership opportunities for women in agriculture.

Youth participation in agriculture

Showing young people that farming can be a modern, viable career.

Local farmer training

Knowledge-sharing and practical training drawn from our own farm.

Sustainable livelihoods

Farming practices that can support families for the long term.

Food security

More quality food grown locally, for local markets.

Agricultural entrepreneurship

Encouraging farming as a business, from planning and records to selling.

Opportunities for smallholder farmers

Exploring how local smallholders could access chicks, incubation, skills and markets.

Working side by side

Growing together, one bed at a time.

On the farm, people plant, learn and share the work. That spirit of togetherness is what we want to grow into a wider community.

Talk to us

These are our intentions and focus areas. As the business grows, we plan to develop more opportunities for community engagement, training and collaboration. Planned activities are not yet established programmes, and we will share real photos and updates as they happen. Development partners interested in this work are welcome to contact us.
